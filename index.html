DJ KaylDownUnder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 September 2026

DJ KaylDownUnder is a local music player. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information handled on your device

The app reads music files and folder names only from a folder you choose. It stores your selected folder, playlists, playback preferences, favourites, and cached track metadata on your device so the player can work. This information is not sent to a server operated by DJ KaylDownUnder.

Optional online metadata lookup

If you choose a metadata-fetch action, the app sends a track name to MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Apple iTunes Search, and Deezer in turn to look for matching track details and album artwork. Those services process requests under their own privacy policies. You can avoid this transmission by not using metadata fetch.

Sharing, advertising, and accounts

DJ KaylDownUnder does not require an account, display advertising, use analytics or advertising SDKs, or sell personal information.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

App preferences and cached metadata remain on your device until you clear app storage or remove the relevant files. The app only has access to the music folder you select.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
